Adventures in Fire Island

My trip to Fire Island was everything I needed — miles of quiet beach, towering dunes, and the kind of stillness you only find far from the mainland. Highlights included biking the boardwalks through the Sunken Forest, watching the sunset from the lighthouse, and spotting deer wandering right up to the trail's edge. No cars, no rush — just sand, salt air, and time to slow down.
